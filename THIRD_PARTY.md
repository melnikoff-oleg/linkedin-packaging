`references/` contains saved public-profile banners, portraits, headlines, and design analysis used to explain the ten composition references. Original photographs, designs, names, and marks remain the property of their respective owners. The profile source links are listed in [reference_accounts.md](reference_accounts.md). These assets are not offered under the MIT license, and no ownership, endorsement, or permission for unrelated reuse is claimed.

`examples/` contains local demonstration designs derived from public professional-profile information and generated/edited imagery. Company names and marks identify the subject's documented work context; they do not imply endorsement by LinkedIn, the subjects, their employers, NP Digital, or the San Diego Padres. Demonstration claims are source-reported, not independently audited. The examples are not a stock-photo or logo library.

Commercial website font files, including Geomanist and Proxima Nova, are excluded from this public source release. The completed example images are raster previews; this repository does not grant a license to redistribute those commercial fonts. The generic company-brand feature accepts a recipient's own locally supplied and appropriately licensed assets.
